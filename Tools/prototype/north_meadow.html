What this prototype is for

  • It runs the data, not a copy of it: grid, terrain costs, stats, weapon ranges and the scripted flee event come from battle_north_meadow.json, characters.json, classes.json, weapons.json, skills.json and terrain.json.
  • It validates the design before any engine code exists — whether the numbers are fun, whether the grid reads, whether 7 enemies against 3 units is the right first fight.
  • Damage has no random variation here, so the preview is exact and the script's promise holds: Rowan's opening hit deals 8. Criticals still fire.
  • It is not the game. No 3D, no animation, no camera work — the real thing is Godot 4 with these rules in C#. Presentation is the part this deliberately leaves out.
